keep books; it does not give tax, legal or financial advice. You are
responsible for your books, your tax returns and your filings.

- Tax rates, codes and return mappings are provided as data that can be wrong
  or out of date. Check them against official sources or with a qualified
  adviser before you file.
- AI agents can misread documents. Ledgers flags low-confidence fields and
  enforces your policy, but you decide what is posted, paid and filed.
